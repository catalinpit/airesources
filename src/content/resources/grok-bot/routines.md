useCase: |
  Learn a job by watching me do it once, then run it as a routine. Follow along as I walk through the multi-step path across my systems, note each tool, step, and decision along the way, and save it as a routine with the inputs it needs. Re-run it on demand or on the schedule I set, show me a run report every time, and stop and ask whenever a step looks different from what you saw. Never skip an approval step that was in the original path. Ask me which job to learn first, how often it should run, and what counts as a successful result, do the first replay with me watching, then save the routine.

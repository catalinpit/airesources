useCase: |
  Keep my digital life tidy, always on. Walk me through connecting Gmail and Google Drive, then configure it: audit my inbox, Drive, and paid subscriptions around the clock, flag newsletters I never open, files I no longer need, and subscriptions I forgot about, and group the recommendations by type. Only discard, unsubscribe, or cancel when I say so, and show me the full list before anything changes. Ask me what must never be touched, how far back to scan, and how often to report, run the first audit with me watching, then save it for a recurring schedule I choose.

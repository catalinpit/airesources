useCase: |
  Stand up a team of bots that coordinate without me in the middle. Create a Researcher, a Writer, and a Chief of Staff, give each a clear lane, and put them in one group chat: the Researcher gathers sources and facts, the Writer turns them into drafts, and the Chief of Staff assigns work, tracks what's in flight, and hands ownership between them. Have them message each other directly, share context in threads, and work in parallel on the shared computer, and only bring me in for decisions or final approval. Ask me what the team's first project is, who owns what, and how often I want a status update, run the first project with me watching the group chat, then save the team.
